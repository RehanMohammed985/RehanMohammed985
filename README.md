## Two things I firmly believe

1. **Obsession can win you the world.** Talent is common and capital is rented. The person who cannot stop thinking about the problem, who ships at 3 AM and re-verifies at 6, beats both. Every edge I have was compounded one obsessive night at a time.

## Multi-agent coordination is my home turf

Most people use AI as autocomplete. I run it like a company, and I've spent serious time on the hard parts of making that true:

- 🕸️ **Orchestration at fleet scale.** Deterministic pipelines fanning out to a dozen concurrent agents and converging through synthesis stages: per-agent structured output schemas, bounded concurrency against rate-limited upstreams, cached resumption so a failed run replays for free instead of burning the budget twice.
- ⚔️ **Adversarial verification layers.** Findings don't graduate on one agent's word. Independent verifier agents attack each claim from different lenses before anything is believed, and every surviving claim carries the primary-source URL it was confirmed against.
- 📚 **Curation under hard rules.** The datasets in my products are agent-built under one constraint: no entry ships unless the underlying primary document was fetched and read first. A tracker, a blog post, or a model's memory is a lead, never a source.
- 🌙 **Autonomous build cycles.** Scheduled agent runs land features on a dev branch before sunrise, and nightly runs test everything twice before releasing to production. I wake up to a changelog, not a to-do list.
- ⚖️ **The governing rule:** agents propose, evidence decides. Nothing ships on a model's word alone.

## What I care about when building

- **Ship daily.** Momentum compounds faster than planning does.
- **Honest software.** A tool that can't check something should say so plainly, never imply it's fine. Most software lies by omission; mine isn't allowed to.
- **Speed is a feature.** If the answer takes ten seconds, it may as well not exist. I engineer for the first result landing in under two.
- **Solo doesn't mean small.** One person with a disciplined agent fleet can research, build, test, and distribute like a team.
